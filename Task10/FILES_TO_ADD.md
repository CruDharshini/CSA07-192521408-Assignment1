# Task 10 – Required files

- `ping_output.txt` – your own ping results to the selected real destination.
- `traceroute_output.txt` – your own traceroute/tracert results.
- `measurement_table.csv` – measured hop/RTT values and notes.
- `connection_capture.pcapng` – your own Wireshark capture.
- `wireshark_headers_labelled.png` – screenshot identifying Ethernet, IP and transport headers.

Use actual outputs from your computer; do not submit sample values as measurements.
